## Abstract

Modern research in the field of transmission and fixation of consciousness states opens up new possibilities for studying cognitive processes and their objectification. This article examines methodological approaches to transmitting consciousness states from one subject to another using advanced **neurointerface** technologies and quantum-resistant blockchain systems. Special attention is paid to the objectification of consciousness states through stable patterns of neural activity, captured using technologies such as **electroencephalography (EEG)**, **magnetoencephalography (MEG)**, and **functional magnetic resonance imaging (fMRI)**. Algorithms of **artificial intelligence (AI)** are applied for processing and transmitting data, enabling classification and analysis of neural signals.

The article also discusses the theoretical aspects of the quantum nature of consciousness, implying the use of quantum processes in the brain to explain cognitive states. The connection of these processes with quantum principles such as **superposition** and **entanglement** provides an opportunity for the application of quantum technologies in transmitting data about the state of consciousness. Within the study, quantum-resistant cryptographic systems are proposed, such as **Quantum Resistant Ledger (QRL)**, which ensure data transmission security and protection against hacking by quantum computers.

The authors deliberately use **neurointerface** rather than *brain-computer interface (BCI)*, because the research focus is on transfer between biologically conditioned subjects, not on brain-computer interaction.

In conclusion, ethical and philosophical aspects of transmitting consciousness states are discussed, as well as the prospects for further research in the integration of technologies and cognitive processes.

## Long-term civilizational vision

> This section is deliberately kept **out of commercial-facing documents** (README, investor decks, pitches to galleries / auction houses / partners). It belongs here because it is part of the scientific and philosophical arc of the whitepaper.

### Inter-species art-as-currency framing

The ASRP authors propose a long-horizon reframe of **SPR-certified artworks** as a proto-unit of exchange for a hypothetical inter-species / inter-civilisational trade system:

- Earth does not produce material goods that advanced civilisations would require, and conventional currency is unlikely to be meaningful to them.
- Their technologies likely surpass ours — a one-for-one technology exchange is implausible.
- **However**, artworks carrying a measurable cognitive imprint (the quantity SPR proposes to certify) could serve as a marker of civilisational maturity — and therefore as a **candidate unit of value** for technology diffusion.
- Such certified works become conceptually analogous to a **gold-and-foreign-currency reserve**, but for an inter-civilisational economy.

### Integration with ecosystem layers

The framing closes on four ecosystem components already present in the README:

| Layer | Role in the vision |
|---|---|
| **ASRP.art + GMS** | SPR-certified works tokenised as Mental Objects in the Global Mental System. |
| **GFS (Global Forecasting System)** | Carries the forecast of humanity's contact with higher / extraterrestrial civilisations — the trigger event for any practical use of the framing. |
| **Kapusta.money** | Quantum-resistant settlement layer; pegs SPR-valued works to a tradeable crypto unit. |
| **Hyperbolic fields (ASRP.drift / FBHFS)** | Hypothesised substrate of an inter-stellar data-exchange network through which such value transfer could take place. |

### Commercial-translation rule

When speaking to galleries, auction houses, collectors, or investors, **this civilisational framing is replaced by practical language**: "measurable asset", "additional valuation layer", "provenance-plus-state". The goal is to first capture the terrestrial art market, then — if and when contact-related events materialise — to have the infrastructure ready.
