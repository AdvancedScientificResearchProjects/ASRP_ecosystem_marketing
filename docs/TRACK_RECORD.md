## Track Record & Case Studies

> Concrete events that prove the ecosystem is not paper.

### 1. UAP repo → U.S. Department of Energy first-reader
- Within hours of publishing `UAP_Reverse_Engineering_Study`, tracked access from a U.S. federal IP range was observed — identified as the **Department of Energy (DoE) / Los Alamos National Laboratory** segment (the same institutional track that historically commissioned IBM-supercomputer simulations on UAP-adjacent research). DoE was **the first federal reader**, landing on the repository nearly immediately after publication.

### 2. Blood-plasma study → Cortical Labs negotiations
- V. Ovseannicova reproduced the hyperbolic-field protocol on blood plasma; raw data passed to analysis → unified public repo.
- Repo initially sent to Cortical Labs (Australian biocomputing company) — no response at first.
- When Cortical Labs announced their neuron-computing game and hype spiked, ASRP posted the plasma study as content on LinkedIn. Cortical Labs engaged, and negotiations are ongoing (last update: Cortical Labs is reviewing the study).

### 3. GFS — early flags on major events
- Tactical nuclear weapon use — early flag matched subsequent public events.
- Additional disclosure-class signals under observation.

### 4. Agricultural Day-4 signal
- Double-blind activation: irradiated seeds show increased volume and moisture vs control.
- Projected scale (estimate): +10% soybean yield → food for an additional ~100 k people per 1 M-person baseline.

### 5. Kosmopoisk / Chernobrov archival cross-reference + Lazar lineage
- `chernobrov-archive` inside UAP study cross-references Vadim Chernobrov's field data (post-landing measurements, **quartz-resonator frequency-deviation instrument** deployed at landing sites, time-dilation measurements with controls) with modern reverse-engineering work.
- **Research lineage note.** Lazar worked directly on intact UAP craft (U.S. government programmes); Kosmopoisk (with Chernobrov) worked only on fragments and landing-site residue, documenting post-landing physical-field anomalies. Both paths converge on the same hyperbolic-field phenomena now investigated by ASRP — Lazar via the craft's power systems, Kosmopoisk via the environmental imprint. Chernobrov's later work documented prototype time-dilation apparatus (with measured deviations), continuing the Lazar-style reverse-engineering path on a materials-limited scale. Denis's relocation within Kazakhstan — to Baikonur (Kyzylorda region) — was an explicit move to continue that research trail.

### 6. Public events — GFS presentations
- **Theta Network Hackathon**, Berlin, 11 Sep 2025 — GFS presented at European conference and hackathon.
- **Industry 4.0 Pitch Battle** at AstanaHub (Digital Bridge 2025) — GFS submitted for industrial pitching; did not pass the selection stage. Application documented on project page.

### 7. DAAT Crystal pilots — bank executives + Forbes-list wearers
- **Bank top-management pilot — testing phase.** Crystals acquired by the executive cohort of a financial institution (institution name undisclosed; under high-load concentration-support rationale).
- **Forbes-list wearers.** Individual wearers include persons from the Forbes list — publicly announced by the founder in a broadcast Telegram channel post on 2026-04-20 (views: 288, forwards: 2). Names not disclosed.
- Both are early pilot-tier signals, not revenue-stage pipelines.

### 8. Neurointerface affiliate sale — proof-of-mechanism for cross-promotion
- ASRP.media ran a banner ad for a neurointerface device embedded in related editorial content; a reader converted through the banner and completed a purchase; ASRP received the affiliate commission. **This is the worked example** underpinning the [Cross-Promotion Pattern](PATHS_TO_MONEY_PRODUCTS.md) — the ecosystem monetisation mechanism is not theoretical.

### 9. NCI SBIR FY26 — Pediatric & Rare Cancers application (Aug 2025 → Oct 2025 systemic cancellation)
- **Application.** On 22 Aug 2025, ASRP CEO Denis Banchenko formally contacted the NIH/NCI Contracting Specialist (via the official NIH SBIR portal) for the NCI SBIR Innovative Concept Award Program under **Notice ID `75N91025R00005`**, proposing a novel therapeutic approach for pediatric and rare cancers based on controlled interactions of hyperbolic fields and space-time curvature. The letter confirmed an existing experimental device prototype, preliminary tests, and opened IP/NDA discussion.
- **Progress.** Registration in the eCPS submission system was completed (screenshot on file, 2 Oct 2025); proposal preparation tracked against the official solicitation checklist (46-page solicitation + Attachment_1 Technical Proposal Form).
- **Outcome.** In October 2025, NCI cancelled solicitation `75N91025R00005` system-wide following **expiration of the federal SBIR/STTR statutory authorization, effective 1 October 2025**. The cancellation was **systemic** — it affected every interested party and was **not based on merit, peer review or technical evaluation** of any submitted proposal. ASRP retains the fully prepared materials for re-submission once SBIR/STTR authorization is restored.
- **Relevance.** This is a documented example of ASRP operating inside formal U.S. federal research-procurement processes. The same scientific position (temporal-desynchronization framing — see [Blood Plasma path](PATHS_TO_MONEY_RESEARCH.md)) is now being carried forward via the Dr. Garry Nolan / Stanford LinkedIn track (see [Outreach Dynamics](OUTREACH.md) below).
