## ⚠️ System-Level Gaps / Пробелы системного уровня

| # | Gap | Impact | Fix |
|---|-----|--------|-----|
| 1 | **No unified lead capture** — 12 funnels but 0 email/CRM integration | Can't nurture across funnels | Deploy single email capture point (Arcanum12th_BOT → email fallback) |
| 2 | **No cross-funnel analytics** — no way to see which funnel feeds which | Can't optimize traffic routing | Tag all ASRP content by funnel ID + set up GA4/Plausible funnel tracking |
| 3 | **3 products with Critical gaps** — fashion, dance, exchange have zero content | Brand promise unfulfilled | Prioritize content creation: 1 article per product as MVP |
| 4 | **No "Funnel Finder" for new visitors** — cold user has no guided path | High bounce rate at asrp.tech | Build 3-question quiz: "What interests you?" → funnel assignment |
| 5 | **Over-reliance on Telegram** — 8 funnels use Telegram as primary nurture | Single point of failure | Add email nurture as secondary channel for all funnels |
| 6 | **No retargeting pixel on any ASRP property** | Can't re-engage cold visitors | Deploy Meta/LI pixel on ASRP.Media + Arcanum12th |
| 7 | **MoF → BoF conversion data unknown** — no tracking of which article leads to which purchase | Blind optimization | Implement UTM tagging + conversion tracking on all course/product links |
